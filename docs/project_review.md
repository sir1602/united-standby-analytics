## 2. Architectural & Analytical Strengths

1. **Empirical Rigor & Zero Silent Imputation (`SC-5`):**
   - Achieves `100.0000%` seat-configuration resolution on performed flights (`814,847 / 814,847`) via the 3-tier `TAIL_OVERRIDE -> FAA_MASTER -> FAA_DEREG` hierarchy in [aircraft.py](file:///home/antigravity/sir1602/united-standby-analytics/src/standby/enrichment/aircraft.py) and [int_aircraft_enriched.sql](file:///home/antigravity/sir1602/united-standby-analytics/dbt/models/intermediate/int_aircraft_enriched.sql), paired with the `faa_tc_max_seats >= 20` Stearman/GA N-number collision guard (`D-19`).
   - Strictly preserves `NULL` on unmatched T-100 flights (e.g., Jul–Aug 2026 lag months) across all estimated load factors, passenger counts, open seats, and SOI scores ([calculations.py](file:///home/antigravity/sir1602/united-standby-analytics/src/standby/enrichment/calculations.py#L120-L128), [assert_sc5_no_silent_t100_imputation.sql](file:///home/antigravity/sir1602/united-standby-analytics/dbt/tests/assert_sc5_no_silent_t100_imputation.sql)).
2. **Leakage-Free SOI Separation (`soi_historical` vs. `soi_planning`):**
   - Clear structural separation between post-hoc `soi_historical` (using realized aircraft, monthly T-100 load factor, and same-day prior disruptions) and leakage-free `soi_planning` (fitted strictly on `train.year < eval.year`, leaving `2019` `100% NULL` and scoring cancelled flights to avoid survivorship bias) across [soi.py](file:///home/antigravity/sir1602/united-standby-analytics/src/standby/scoring/soi.py) and [int_flights_soi_scored.sql](file:///home/antigravity/sir1602/united-standby-analytics/dbt/models/intermediate/int_flights_soi_scored.sql).
3. **Provenance & Confidence Tagging:**
   - Every column in the enriched pipeline is registered in [`COLUMN_CONFIDENCE_TIERS`](file:///home/antigravity/sir1602/united-standby-analytics/src/standby/enrichment/confidence.py#L11-L86) (`actual`, `derived`, `estimated`), and modeled TSA/hour-block adjustments are stored in separate `*_adjusted` columns without overwriting base T-100 estimates (`D-22`).
4. **Atomic, Invariant-Checked Tableau Export:**
   - [tableau.py](file:///home/antigravity/sir1602/united-standby-analytics/src/standby/export/tableau.py) stages all 8 CSVs and [export_metadata.json](file:///home/antigravity/sir1602/united-standby-analytics/exports/tableau/export_metadata.json) into a temporary directory, validates schema/null/bound/referential invariants, and atomically swaps files into `exports/tableau/`.
5. **Fast, Secret-Free CI:**
   - [.github/workflows/ci.yml](file:///home/antigravity/sir1602/united-standby-analytics/.github/workflows/ci.yml) runs end-to-end in under 60 seconds without requiring live GCP credentials.

## 4. Methodology, Statistical Calibration & BI Critique

1. **`S_disp` Scale & Variance Mismatch Between `soi_historical` and `soi_planning`:**
   - In [phase4_validation_summary.json](file:///home/antigravity/sir1602/united-standby-analytics/docs/phase4_validation_summary.json), `s_disp_hist` uses `disp_hist_cap = 2.0` on realized same-day disruptions. Because the cancellation rate is ~1.94%, ~95%+ of flights have zero prior same-day disruptions, pegging `s_disp_hist` near 100 (baseline mean **`99.16`**).
   - By contrast, `s_disp_plan` uses an 8× tighter cap (`disp_plan_cap = 0.25`) on expected disruption $\hat{D}_{\text{plan}}$, yielding a baseline mean of **`80.69`** and dropping to **`48.49`** in `late_jan_midweek` (vs. `97.52` historical).
   - **Impact:** This drives a systematic ~3.6-point downward shift in `soi_planning` (`58.09` vs. `61.73` overall; `58.27` vs. `66.75` in 2026) and makes Pillar 3's `disruption_heavy` weight sensitivity (`ρ = 0.994` on `soi_historical`) look artificially stable because `s_disp_hist` has almost no variance.
   - Furthermore, [`validate_pillar3_weight_sensitivity`](file:///home/antigravity/sir1602/united-standby-analytics/src/standby/validation/soi_validation.py#L549-L553) excludes `demand_heavy` from the automated stability gate (`_CORE_SENSITIVITY_SCHEMES`) and only tests `soi_historical`, never `soi_planning`.
2. **Holiday Prior Granularity (`soi_planning` Non-Monotonicity):**
   - In Pillar 2 ([phase4_validation_summary.json](file:///home/antigravity/sir1602/united-standby-analytics/docs/phase4_validation_summary.json#L87-L120)), `soi_planning` scores `thanksgiving_sunday` (`55.19`) *higher* than `christmas_peak` (`53.70`) because Tier 1 of the TSA prior groups by `(month, iso_dow, is_holiday_window)` — averaging Dec 1 (Thanksgiving Sunday) with Dec 22/29 (Christmas Sundays). Keying Tier 1 by `(holiday_name, iso_dow)` (or day-offset within the holiday window) would better separate peak return days.
